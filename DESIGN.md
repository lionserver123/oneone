## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the ethos of a thoughtful interior architecture practice: where modernist restraint intersects with warm, material-driven tactile quietude. Influenced by modern Japanese and Taiwanese spatial sensibilities, the visual language respects void, silence, and tectonic precision. 

The aesthetic is characterized as **Architectural Editorial Minimalism**:
- **Poetic Structure:** Clean alignment, quiet rhythm, and deliberate asymmetry evoke physical spatial progression.
- **Tectonic Honesty:** Visual elements behave like authentic building materials—linen-toned creams, mineral plasters, weathered slate, and warm, unglazed terracotta.
- **Atmospheric Serenity:** Generous margins, breathability, and disciplined typographic hierarchy cultivate stillness without feeling cold or clinical.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from tactile matter—raw clay, natural limestone, lime wash, and charred wood.

- **Primary (`#d64a08` - Terracotta):** Represents raw craft, kiln-fired clay, and focused focal points. Used sparingly for interactive highlights, curated accent lines, active indicators, and critical calls-to-action.
- **Secondary (`#a8a29a` - Architectural Stone):** Evokes honed granite and lime plaster. Applied to secondary typography, subtle structure lines, structural metadata, and muted states.
- **Tertiary / Base Environment (`#f3efe5` - Warm Cream Canvas):** The foundational substrate of the environment. Unlike sterile white, it casts a natural, paper-and-linen warmth across screens.
- **Neutral Surface & Deep Pigment (`#2b2a29` - Deep Warm Charcoal):** Used for authoritative typography, high-contrast structural framing, and grounding architectural elements.
- **Subordinate Surfaces:** Pure White (`#ffffff`) and Soft Plaster (`#f8f6f0`) serve as elevated framing surfaces and clean mats for architectural photography and spatial portfolio sheets.

## Typography

The typographical treatment reflects an editorial monograph. 

- **Display & Headlines (`Noto Serif`):** Delivers quiet authority, classic literary poise, and architectural dignity. Line heights remain generous to ensure multi-line headings read like gallery wall-text.
- **Body & Structural Information (`Inter`):** Engineered for legibility and modernist clarity, rendered in light and regular weights (`300` and `400`). Ensures technical floorplan details, material specs, and editorial prose remain effortless to read.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** High tracking (`0.12em` to `0.16em`) rendered in all caps or disciplined small caps to emulate architectural title blocks and drafting blueprints.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an architectural 12-column modular grid with generous negative space acting as a physical pause.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `margin: 3rem` (expanding up to `5rem` on ultra-wide screens) and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Layouts alternate between full-bleed photographic reveals and tightly framed editorial text blocks spanning 4 to 8 columns.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`. Elements stack into balanced multi-tiered arrangements.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Fluid vertical narrative pacing with dedicated safe margins.
- **Rhythm & Empty Space:** Sections use open pacing (`space-xl` and larger multiples) to honor the architectural concept of *Ma* (間)—the conscious use of void to give presence to form.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial layering relies on physical construction techniques rather than synthetic skeuomorphic drop shadows.

- **Low-Contrast Outlines & Hairlines:** Instead of heavy dropped shadows, layers are separated by 1px architectural joints (`border-color: rgba(43, 42, 41, 0.12)` or `rgba(168, 162, 154, 0.35)`).
- **Tonal Mats & Inset Surfaces:** Layer depth is formed by nesting surfaces: an overarching `#f3efe5` canvas holding elevated `#f8f6f0` modules or `#ffffff` photographic mats.
- **Whisper Ambient Shadow:** When interactive float is mandatory (e.g., sticky site navigation or active lightbox modals), depth is achieved through an ultra-soft, diffused shadow: `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -12px rgba(43, 42, 41, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The geometric ethos is unapologetically tectonic: **Level 0 (Sharp)**.

- **Crisp Edges:** All containers, buttons, cards, image frames, and interactive fields employ an unrounded `0px` radius.
- **Architectural Reference:** Emulates cut stone, milled timber, steel framing, and drafted architectural plans. Rounded shapes are strictly avoided to preserve the studio's disciplined aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#2b2a29` background with `#f3efe5` text, or accent `#d64a08` with white text. Zero border radius. Padding: `0.875rem 2rem`. Text style: `label-md` with uppercase tracking. Hover transitions smoothly into a delicate tint shift or reverse outline.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with a 1px solid border (`#2b2a29` or `#a8a29a`), foreground `#2b2a29`. Hover fills the background with `#2b2a29` and turns text to `#f3efe5`.
- **Text Link:** Simple underline offset by `4px`, transitioning to `#d64a08` on hover.

### Cards & Project Folios
- Borderless or bounded by hairline 1px `#a8a29a` dividers.
- Photography takes precedence with a strict aspect ratio (e.g., 4:3 or 16:10).
- Metadata labels sit beneath or above images in `label-sm`, accompanied by project titles in `headline-sm` or `title-lg`.

### Input Fields
- Underlined-only (drafting style) or boxed in a 1px hairline border (`#a8a29a`).
- Background: `#f8f6f0` or transparent.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#d64a08` with zero outer ring glow.
- Placeholder text: Muted `#a8a29a`, text style `body-md`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Sharp 0px corners for checkboxes; true geometric circles for radios.
- Inactive: 1px outline in `#a8a29a`.
- Active: Solid `#d64a08` fill with clean architectural glyphs.

### Chips & Badges
- Sharp rectangular tags with `padding: 0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- Background: `#f8f6f0` with a 1px `#a8a29a` border or tonal `#d64a08` fill for featured statuses.
- Typography: `label-sm`.

### Studio-Specific Components
- **Material Swatch Drawer:** Rectangular split tiles showing stone, wood, metal, and plaster textures with inline typographic specs.
- **Architectural Spec Table:** Tabular, ruled lines spanning column widths with mono-spaced coordinate numbers and technical labels.
- **Fullscreen Spatial Viewer:** Floating minimalistic framing controls in `#ffffff` over dark photographic viewports.
